SECTION 06 / ABOUT

I started writing software because I wanted to understand how ideas become products.

That curiosity turned into a practice. I started in 2020 building interfaces for e-commerce, delivery and contact-center products, and the pattern repeated: the interesting part was never the framework, it was the gap between what someone needed and what a system actually did.

Since then I have worked across the whole stack, taking products from a first schema to a deployed URL. I like problems where the constraints are real — a business with a deadline, a team without a developer, a process that has outgrown its spreadsheet. I work from Bogotá with international teams, and I care about writing software that stays understandable long after it ships.
